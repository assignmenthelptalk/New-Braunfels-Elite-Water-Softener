### What Drives the Cost of a Water Softener?

Eight factors set the price of a softener installed in New Braunfels:

- **System type:** salt-based, salt-free, [dual tank](/dual-tank-water-softener/) or a softener paired with a filter or reverse osmosis.
- **Capacity:** larger households and harder water need more resin and a larger tank.
- **Brand and valve:** control valve quality and warranty vary widely.
- **Installation conditions:** distance to a drain, access to the main line and the condition of existing plumbing.
- **Plumbing extras:** bypass valve, shut-off valve and pressure regulator if pressure is high.
- **Permits:** local rules for plumbing work. Ask your installer whether the City of New Braunfels requires a permit for your job.
- **Add-ons:** sediment or carbon filtration, reverse osmosis at the kitchen tap.
- **Warranty and service:** longer coverage and maintenance plans add to the upfront price.

NBU's 2018 water quality report put average hardness at 15.1 grains per gallon, about 258 mg/L, so many New Braunfels homes need mid-to-large capacity units.

### What Does It Cost to Run a Water Softener?

Running costs are small compared with the purchase but they recur. Expect salt or potassium chloride refills, a modest amount of water for each regeneration cycle, a small amount of electricity and an annual service. Potassium chloride costs more per bag than salt. Newer control heads regenerate by actual water use, which trims salt and water. For a four-person household at 15 grains per gallon, salt use works out to roughly 40 pounds a month, assuming about 3,500 grains removed per pound of salt, plus the water used for regeneration.

### What a Water Softener Quote Includes

A good quote lists the same items every time, so you can compare. Look for:

1. System type and grain capacity.
2. Control valve model and brand.
3. A bypass valve, drain connection and air gap.
4. Any pressure regulator or shut-off valve work.
5. Permit costs, if required.
6. Warranty terms for the tank, resin, valve and labor.
7. The installer's licence number: a TCEQ Water Treatment Specialist licence or a Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners licence.
8. A statement of what is not included.

### How to Compare Quotes

Compare capacity first, since a cheap unit that is too small costs more in salt and wears out faster. Then compare warranty length and what it covers, then the labor scope. The lowest price is rarely the best value if it leaves out the bypass valve, the drain connection or a licence you can verify.
