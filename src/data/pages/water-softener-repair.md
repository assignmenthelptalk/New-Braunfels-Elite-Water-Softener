## What Water Softener Repairs Are Available in New Braunfels, TX?

Seven repairs cover the common failures: control valve repair, motor replacement, float valve repair, injector cleaning, head replacement, brine line repair and leak repair.

<div class="svc-grid">

<div class="svc-card">

### Water Softener Control Valve Repair

The control valve directs water through the service, backwash, brine and rinse stages. When it sticks, leaks or fails to cycle, water stays hard or runs constantly to the drain. We rebuild the valve with new seals and spacers, or replace it when it is worn beyond repair.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![Water Softener Motor Replacement New Braunfels TX](/placeholders/card.svg)

### Water Softener Motor Replacement

The motor turns the valve through its cycle. A failed motor leaves the unit stuck in one stage, and you hear a hum or grinding. We replace the motor and reset the cycle.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![Float Valve Repair New Braunfels TX](/placeholders/card.svg)

### Float Valve Repair

The float valve limits how much water enters the brine tank. A stuck float causes overfilling, weak brine or salt that will not dissolve. We clean or replace the float assembly and set the brine level.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![Injector Cleaning New Braunfels TX](/placeholders/card.svg)

### Injector Cleaning

The injector draws brine into the resin tank. Sediment clogs it, and a blocked injector means no brine reaches the resin, so water stays hard. We remove, clean and test the injector, and replace it if it is damaged.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![Head Replacement New Braunfels TX](/placeholders/card.svg)

### Head Replacement

The control head holds the timer, display and electronics. When it loses its settings, shows no display or stops responding, replacing the head restores the system without a new tank.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![Brine Line Repair New Braunfels TX](/placeholders/card.svg)

### Brine Line Repair

The brine line carries brine from the tank to the valve. Cracks, kinks or loose fittings cause air leaks and failed regeneration. We replace damaged tubing and tighten or replace fittings.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![Water Softener Leak Repair New Braunfels TX](/placeholders/card.svg)

### Water Softener Leak Repair

Leaks occur at the control head, drain line, tank connections or bypass valve. We trace the source, replace the faulty seal, fitting or hose and test for leaks. A leaking or cracked tank usually means replacement.

</div>

## Common Water Softener Problems We Fix

- **Not regenerating:** the unit never starts a cycle because of a power, timer, motor or valve fault.
- **Salt bridge:** a hard crust forms above the water in the brine tank, so salt cannot dissolve. We break it up and clean the tank.
- **Mushing:** salt dissolves and re-forms into sludge at the bottom, blocking brine flow. We empty and clean the tank.
- **Error codes:** meanings differ by brand. We read the code, find the cause and clear it.

 Tell us the make and model when you call so we can confirm we can service it.

## Is it time to repair or replace?

Repair fits a young unit with one failed part. Replacement fits an old unit, a leaking tank or repeated failures. Our free inspection compares both prices.

<div class="faq-list">

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Why is my water still hard even though the softener has salt?</summary>

Salt alone does not soften water. A clogged injector, fouled resin, a failed valve or a missed regeneration cycle can all leave hard water.

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>How do I fix a water softener that is not regenerating?</summary>

Check power and the control head settings first. If the unit still does not cycle, the motor, valve or head needs repair, and a technician diagnoses which.

<summary>What causes a salt bridge?</summary>

Humidity and salt type cause a crust to form above the water in the brine tank. Breaking the crust and cleaning the tank restores brine.
