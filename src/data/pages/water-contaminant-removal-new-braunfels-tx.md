## Which Contaminants Can a Filter Remove in New Braunfels, TX?

Six filter types target specific contaminants: lead and arsenic, chloramine and chlorine, fluoride, PFAS, nitrate and volatile organic compounds.

### Lead and Arsenic Removal

Lead enters drinking water mainly from old plumbing, and the EPA sets its health goal for lead at zero. Arsenic occurs naturally in some groundwater, and the EPA limit is 10 micrograms per liter. Under-sink reverse osmosis systems and certified filters reduce both. We recommend filters certified to the relevant NSF/ANSI standard and a test before and after installation.

</div>

### Chloramine and Chlorine Removal

Chlorine and chloramine are disinfectants that can affect taste and smell. Standard carbon removes chlorine well but works slowly on chloramine, so catalytic carbon is used for chloramine. NBU reports its disinfectant residual as total chlorine, averaging 2.58 ppm in 2025, and listed it as chloramines in its 2019 to 2022 reports, so catalytic carbon is the safer choice for taste and smell.

### Fluoride Filter Installation

Fluoride occurs naturally in some groundwater, and the EPA enforceable limit is 4.0 milligrams per liter. Reverse osmosis and activated alumina media reduce fluoride. We install the type that suits your flow and budget.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">

![PFAS Water Filter New Braunfels TX](https://placehold.co/660x440/1A3C5E/FFFFFF.png?text=PFAS+Water+Filter+photo+placeholder)

### PFAS Water Filter

PFAS are man-made chemicals. In 2024 the EPA set limits of 4 parts per trillion for PFOA and PFOS in public water systems. Activated carbon, ion exchange and reverse osmosis can reduce PFAS, and results depend on the product. [Verify current EPA limits before launch]

</div>

<div class="svc-card">

![Nitrate Filter Installation New Braunfels TX](https://placehold.co/660x440/1A3C5E/FFFFFF.png?text=Nitrate+Filter+Installation+photo+placeholder)

### Nitrate Filter Installation

Nitrate comes from fertilizer, septic systems and livestock, and the EPA limit is 10 milligrams per liter as nitrogen. It matters most for infants and for private wells in farming areas. Ion exchange and reverse osmosis reduce nitrate, and boiling water does not.

</div>

### VOC (Volatile Organic Compound) Filter

Volatile organic compounds are chemicals from fuels, solvents and industrial sources. Activated carbon is the standard treatment. A lab test confirms whether VOCs are present before you spend on a filter.

## When to Test for Contaminants

Test your water if you have a private well, an infant in the home, old plumbing, a change in taste or smell or a nearby source such as farming, fuel storage or industry. NBU's 2025 report describes water that leaves the system, and most of the supply is Guadalupe River water with Edwards Aquifer and Trinity Aquifer groundwater added. A test at your tap shows what reaches you. Ask which tests are free and which need an accredited lab, and what each costs.

> **Not a good fit if...** you have no test result. We do not sell a filter for a problem a test has not found.


## How do you know which contaminant matters?
