### Commercial Water Softener System Types

Commercial systems come in four common forms:

- **Single-tank high-capacity units** for steady, moderate demand.
- **Dual tank systems** that alternate tanks for continuous softened water around the clock.
- **High-flow systems** with larger valves for sites that draw a lot of water at once.
- **Softener plus filter combinations** that also remove sediment and chlorine before equipment.

The right type depends on peak flow in gallons per minute, daily volume and how long equipment can tolerate hard water. A technician measures these before recommending anything. ### Industries We Serve in New Braunfels

New Braunfels businesses in many sectors rely on soft water. Typical commercial users include restaurants and cafes, hotels, laundromats and laundries, car washes, medical and dental offices, and multi-unit housing. Each has equipment that scales faster in hard water: steamers, dishwashers, boilers and spray nozzles.

NBU's supply is mostly Guadalupe River water with Edwards Aquifer and Trinity Aquifer groundwater added. NBU's 2018 water quality report put average hardness at 15.1 grains per gallon, about 258 mg/L, which the U.S. Geological Survey rates as very hard.

### Commercial vs. Residential Water Softeners

Commercial units differ from residential ones in four ways: they handle higher flow rates, hold more resin, run around the clock and usually come with a service schedule. A residential unit placed on a commercial load wears out fast and lets hard water through, so sizing from peak flow is the first step.

### Commercial Installation Process

1. **Site visit and water test.** We measure flow, pressure and hardness and review the equipment to protect.
2. **Written proposal.** You receive a sized system and an itemized price.
3. **Installation.** A qualified technician installs the unit with a bypass valve and drain, scheduled to limit downtime. Ask for the technician's TCEQ or plumbing-board licence number and proof of insurance before the work starts.
4. **Handover and support.** We show your staff how to check salt and read the control head.

### Maintenance Plans

Commercial softeners need salt, cleaning and inspection on a regular schedule so equipment stays protected. Ask what each visit includes and how often it is scheduled. Plans keep the system at capacity and catch problems before they cause downtime.

> **Not a good fit if...** your site is a very small office with little water use. A residential-size unit costs less and works fine.


## Which checks decide the right commercial system?

Check three things: peak flow in gallons per minute, which equipment needs protection first and how much downtime you can accept during installation. A free site review settles all three.



### Frequently Asked Questions

**How do you size a commercial water softener?**
We size it from peak flow rate and daily water use, plus the hardness of your water. Household size does not apply.

**Can installation happen without shutting down my business?**
Most installs need a short water shutoff. We schedule around your busiest hours to limit downtime.

**What equipment does a commercial softener protect?**
It protects boilers, water heaters, dishwashers, ice machines, steamers and plumbing from scale buildup.

**Do you offer service contracts?**
Ask whether service is offered by contract or per visit. The proposal states what is included.

**Does a commercial system need filtration too?**
Often yes. Sediment and chlorine can harm resin and equipment, and a test shows what your water carries.
