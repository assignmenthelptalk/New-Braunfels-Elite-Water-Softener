### Whole-House Water Softener Installation

A whole-house water softener is a single unit installed on the main water line that treats every cold and hot water fixture in the home. The technician sizes the unit from your household size, daily water use and a hardness reading, then sets the control head to regenerate on a schedule that matches your usage. A correctly sized unit softens water without running out between regenerations or wasting salt on cycles it does not need.

The installation includes the softener tank, brine tank, control valve, drain connection and a test at every tap before we leave. For homes served by the city, the unit sits after the main shut-off. For homes on a private well, it sits after the pressure tank, and a water test first confirms whether iron or sulfur need separate treatment. NBU's supply is mostly Guadalupe River water with Edwards Aquifer and Trinity Aquifer groundwater added, and it arrives hard, so a water softener installation protects the water heater and plumbing along the whole line.

### Portable Water Softener Installation

A portable water softener is an exchange-tank unit that serves temporary, seasonal or rental situations where a permanent system is not practical. The tank is swapped on a schedule instead of regenerating in the home. Portable units soften water for one household without permanent plumbing changes, though a whole-house system costs less to run over the long term for a home you own.

### Bypass Valve Installation

A bypass valve is a set of valves that routes water around the softener, so you can service or replace the unit without shutting off water to the house. We install a bypass valve on every softener we place. It also lets you send untreated water to outdoor taps or when the unit is being maintained.

### Softener Plumbing Connections

Plumbing connections join the softener to your home's supply, drain and, where needed, a water loop. Three connections matter most:

- **Supply and return lines** run from the main line into the softener and back out, using shut-off valves so the unit can be isolated.
- **The drain line** carries regeneration water to a floor drain, standpipe or laundry tub with an air gap that prevents wastewater from backing into the unit.
- **A softener loop** is an optional branch that keeps selected outdoor lines on unsoftened water.

Where your home has no suitable drain or supply stub near the unit, the technician adds the new line during the same visit.

### Pressure Regulator and Shut-Off Valve Installation

A pressure regulator keeps household water pressure within the range your softener's manufacturer allows, and many plumbing codes cap static pressure at 80 psi. Excess pressure shortens the life of softeners, water heaters and fixtures. When we measure high pressure at your main, we install a regulator with the softener. A new water shut-off valve at the main gives you a reliable way to stop water to the house in an emergency.


## 4-Step Water Softener Installation Process

1. **Free consultation.** We learn your household size and what you notice at the tap, and book a visit.
2. **Water testing and recommendation.** A technician tests hardness, checks pressure and the install location, and gives an itemized, written price.
3. **Professional installation.** The unit is set, plumbed with a bypass valve, drained with an air gap and programmed, then tested at every tap. A standard install typically takes about 2 to 4 hours.
4. **Ongoing support.** We show you how to add salt and read the control head, and we stay available for service.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does water softener installation take in New Braunfels?**
Most installations finish in a single visit. Most standard installs take about 2 to 4 hours. The technician confirms the schedule at your estimate, based on plumbing access, drain location and system type.

**Do I need a permit to install a water softener in New Braunfels?**
Permit rules depend on the plumbing work involved. Ask whether the City of New Braunfels requires a permit for your job. We confirm what applies to your address before the install.

**Can you install a water softener on a private well?**
Yes. Well installations go after the pressure tank, and a water test first checks for iron, sulfur and sediment that need their own filters. Treating those first protects the resin in the softener.

**Where in my house does the softener go?**
The unit goes on the main water line, usually in a garage, utility room or by the water heater, near a drain and an electrical outlet. Outdoor spigots can stay on unsoftened water through a loop if you prefer.

**Is a bypass valve necessary?**
A bypass valve is standard on every installation we do. It lets you isolate the softener for service while the rest of the house keeps water.
