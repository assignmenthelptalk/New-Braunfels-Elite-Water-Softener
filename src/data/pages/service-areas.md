## Why Water Varies From Town to Town

Each town draws from a different mix of river, lake and aquifer water, so hardness changes with the address. The City of San Marcos reported 16 gpg in 2024, NBU reported 15.1 gpg for New Braunfels in 2018, and the Canyon Lake Shores system reported 10.9 to 19.5 gpg in 2018. Seguin and Wimberley publish no hardness figure in their reports. A free water test at your tap gives the number that matters.
