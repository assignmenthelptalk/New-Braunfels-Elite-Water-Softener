### Why Softener Size Matters

Size is the capacity of the resin to hold hardness minerals before it must regenerate, measured in grains. A correctly sized unit softens water between regenerations, regenerates only when needed and uses salt and water efficiently. An undersized unit runs out early, regenerates too often and wears the resin faster. An oversized unit costs more, regenerates rarely and can let the resin sit stale between cycles.

### The Sizing Formula

Water softener sizing uses three numbers:

1. **People in the home**
2. **Daily water use per person:** about 75 to 80 gallons is a common sizing assumption.
3. **Water hardness in grains per gallon (gpg)**

Multiply them to get the grains your household uses each day, then multiply by the number of days you want between regenerations.

**Daily grains = people x gallons per person x hardness (gpg)**

### Worked Example for a New Braunfels Home

NBU's 2018 water quality report put average hardness at 15.1 grains per gallon, about 258 mg/L. Use 15 gpg as an example, and replace it with your own test result.

- Four people at 80 gallons each use 320 gallons a day.
- 320 gallons x 15 gpg = 4,800 grains a day.
- For about 7 days between regenerations: 4,800 x 7 = 33,600 grains.
- The next standard size up is a 48,000-grain unit, which leaves a margin.

### Adjust for Iron and High Use

Iron makes the resin work harder. As a rule, add roughly 3 to 5 grains per gallon to your hardness for every 1 part per million of iron in the water. Check your softener manufacturer's sizing guide for the exact adjustment. ### Common Water Softener Sizes

| Size (grains) | Typical household |
|---|---|
| 24,000 | 1 to 3 people, moderate hardness |
| 32,000 | 3 to 4 people, moderate to high hardness |
| 48,000 | 4 to 6 people, high hardness or iron |
| 64,000 | 6 or more people, very hard water or heavy use |

These ranges are guides, since your test result and daily use change the answer. At NBU's 2018 average of 15 gpg, a four-person household using roughly 300 gallons a day removes about 4,500 grains daily. On paper a 32,000-grain unit then regenerates about every 7 days, and in practice sooner because part of the rated capacity is held in reserve. Softeners generally operate at 20 to 125 PSI and 34°F to 100°F, so check pressure and location before you choose.

> **Not a good fit if...** you want to pick a size without a water test. Hardness and iron vary by address, and the formula needs your actual numbers.
