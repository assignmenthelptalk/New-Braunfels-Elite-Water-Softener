## Why Canyon Lake Residents Need a Water Softener

Canyon Lake water is hard, and it varies more than most. The Canyon Lake Water Service Company report for its Canyon Lake Shores system (TX0460019) listed total hardness of 187 to 333 mg/L in 2018, which is 10.9 to 19.5 grains per gallon. Calcium reached 98.1 mg/L and magnesium 47.5 mg/L in the same report. <mark class="data-gap">[VERIFY: the current average hardness; the report on file is the 2018 edition]</mark>

The wide range follows the sources. The report states that groundwater is generally harder than surface water, and the system draws on both. At 10.9 gpg, a household of four using 320 gallons a day removes about 3,490 grains daily and a 48,000-grain softener regenerates about every 14 days. At 19.5 gpg the load is about 6,240 grains daily, and the same unit regenerates about every 8 days.

## About Canyon Lake Water Quality

The Canyon Lake Shores system takes surface water from Canyon Lake Reservoir at two treatment plants and adds groundwater from the Trinity Aquifer. The company, formerly Canyon Lake Water Service Company, now trades as The Texas Water Company. Its 2018 report listed a chlorine residual that averaged 1.52 ppm, against a limit of 4 ppm. Smaller systems such as Canyon Lake Estates Water Supply and Westhaven WSC serve some subdivisions, so check your water bill to see which utility supplies your address.

Homes on private wells are not covered by any utility report. A well owner tests for bacteria every year and for hardness, nitrate and other substances as local conditions call for.
