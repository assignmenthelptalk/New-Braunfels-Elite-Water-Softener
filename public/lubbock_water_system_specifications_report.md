## 1. City of Lubbock Water Quality Baseline (Item #4)

- **Total Hardness:** Standardized to **9.9 to 11.2 gpg** (approximately **170 to 192 mg/L** or ppm), aligning with recent City of Lubbock Consumer Confidence Reports (CCR).
- **Total Dissolved Solids (TDS):** Registers at approximately **620 mg/L (ppm)**, highlighting a high mineral payload that justifies a whole-house softener paired with a point-of-use Reverse Osmosis (RO) system.
- **pH Range:** Slightly alkaline, averaging around **8.1 pH**.
- **Disinfectant:** Chloramines / Free Chlorine (ranging from 1.0 to 2.0 mg/L).
